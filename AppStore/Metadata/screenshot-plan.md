# Screenshot capture plan

Apple currently requires one to ten screenshots per supported device family. Lippi targets both iPhone and iPad, so prepare at least one accepted iPhone 6.9-inch image and one 13-inch iPad image for every submitted localization.

## Accepted master sizes

- iPhone 6.9-inch portrait: `1260 × 2736`, `1290 × 2796`, or `1320 × 2868`
- iPad 13-inch portrait: `2064 × 2752` or `2048 × 2732`
- Format: PNG or JPEG, without alpha

## Recommended six-screen story

1. **Today** — “Your day, gently organized”
2. **Smart Goals** — “A roadmap built around your reality”
3. **Roadmap result** — “Clear milestones. Useful next actions.”
4. **Voice assistant** — “Ask naturally. Keep moving.”
5. **Focus and Live Activity** — “Focus without losing the wider day”
6. **Health and privacy** — “Wellbeing context that stays on device”

Create separate Russian and English captures with real localized UI. Do not fabricate screens, show placeholder model states, expose personal HealthKit values, or add device frames that obscure the app.

The simulator was intentionally not launched during release preparation. Capture these images manually after opening the exact Release candidate and verifying the final UI.
