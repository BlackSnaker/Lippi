## Description

Lippi brings focus, thoughtful planning, and everyday wellbeing together in one private iPhone workspace.

START WITH WHAT MATTERS

See your priorities, active tasks, progress, and next focus session in a clear Today view. Organize tasks without turning your day into another source of pressure.

BUILD SMARTER ROADMAPS

Turn a meaningful goal into a practical sequence of milestones, first actions, success criteria, and helpful check-in questions. Lippi’s Bonsai model runs on your device after a one-time model download, so your goal text and generated roadmap stay local.

ASK IN YOUR OWN WORDS

The context-aware voice assistant understands different ways of phrasing the same request. Start focus sessions, navigate Lippi, manage tasks, and open Smart Goals with a natural command.

FIND A SUSTAINABLE RHYTHM

Use Pomodoro timers, Live Activities, widgets, breathing practices, eye exercises, and short breaks to protect attention without losing momentum.

OPTIONAL HEALTH CONTEXT

With your permission, Lippi can use selected Apple Health and Apple Watch signals to suggest a gentler focus pace. Health information is processed on device and remains optional.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No advertising SDKs
• No third-party analytics
• No cross-app tracking
• Local account and local plans
• On-device AI and neural voice
• Clear system permissions and controls

Lippi supports general productivity and wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

The Bonsai intelligence model and neural voice model require separate downloads after installation. Availability and speed depend on your device, free storage, and network connection.
