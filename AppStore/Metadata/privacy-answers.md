# App Privacy answers

These answers are based on a source and dependency audit for version 1.0 (build 2). Reconfirm them if networking, analytics, cloud sync, advertising, or account infrastructure changes.

## Tracking

- Does the app or its third-party partners use data for tracking? **No**
- Tracking domains: **None**

## Data collection

- Does the developer collect data from this app? **No**

Lippi stores account details, tasks, goals, progress, preferences, and permitted HealthKit context on the user’s device. The developer does not receive or retain this information.

Model downloads and curated public-reference requests may expose routine network metadata, such as an IP address, to the destination host. Lippi does not attach goal text, health information, account details, or voice recordings to those requests.

## Health and fitness

HealthKit data is read only after Apple’s permission flow. It is processed on device for general wellbeing suggestions. Completed breathing practices may be written to HealthKit as mindful sessions. HealthKit data is not sent to the developer or an external AI provider.

## Contact information and user content

The local account’s email, name, password hash, tasks, goals, and voice-command results remain on device and therefore are not “collected” by the developer under App Store privacy-label definitions.
